# ---------- A: TEACHING ----------
# Type maths with *asterisks* for italic letters and ² ³ for powers, for example *x*² + 7*x* + 12
# **Double asterisks** make bold. <br> starts a new line inside a step.
examples:
  - question: "Simplify 4*a* + 3*b* + 2*a* − *b*"
    steps:
      - "Find the like terms (terms with the same letter). Each term keeps the sign in front of it:<br>4*a* and 2*a*<br>3*b* and −*b*"
      - "Simplify the like terms:<br>4*a* + 2*a* = 6*a*<br>3*b* − *b* = 2*b*"
      - "Put all the terms together to write your answer."
    result: "6*a* + 2*b*"
  - question: "Simplify 5*x* + 2*y* − 3*x* + 4 − *y* + 6"
    steps:
      - "Find the like terms (terms with the same letter, and numbers on their own). Each term keeps the sign in front of it:<br>5*x* and −3*x*<br>2*y* and −*y*<br>4 and 6"
      - "Simplify the like terms:<br>5*x* − 3*x* = 2*x*<br>2*y* − *y* = *y*<br>4 + 6 = 10"
      - "Put all the terms together to write your answer."
    result: "2*x* + *y* + 10"
